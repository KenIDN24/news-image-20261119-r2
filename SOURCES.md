Every story on this page was reported on Thursday 19 November 2026 and

## Leads

**1. Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — The leaders of the
United States, China and Russia met at Shenzhen's Kylin Villa on the
sidelines of the APEC summit, the first three-way meeting of the three
powers. Trump and Xi held a bilateral first, then the trilateral. The
agenda covered nuclear arms control (the New START treaty expired in
February 2026), the Iran war and trade; no agreement was expected.
— Reuters, Le Monde, AJP and the New York Times, 19 Nov round-ups.

**2. Israeli tanks cross Gaza ceasefire line** — Israeli tanks and
ground forces crossed the "Yellow Line" ceasefire line in central
Gaza, advancing east of the Nuseirat and Bureij camps — the first
ground advance since the October 2025 truce. The IDF said it was
dismantling a tunnel that threatened its troops; Hamas called the move
a flagrant violation and at least three people were killed in strikes.
— Al Jazeera and wire reporting, 19 Nov.

**3. Jeffries nominated for Speaker, 251–11** — US House Democrats
nominated Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker in a closed-door vote of 251–11
on Wednesday 18 November. Jeffries is poised to become the first Black
Speaker; Democrats are projected to hold 259 House seats, their
largest majority in 35 years. The floor vote falls on 3 January 2027
and needs 218 votes.
— AP and CNN, 18–19 Nov.

## Grid cards (4–12)

**4. Anthropic IPO is biggest ever: $81.3bn** — Anthropic priced its
IPO at $83 a share, above the $70–78 range, selling 980 million shares
to raise $81.3 billion — the largest IPO in history — at a fully
diluted valuation of about $1.5 trillion. It debuted on the Nasdaq on
19 November under the ticker ANT.
— Financial and wire reporting, 19 Nov.

**5. Crimea bridge hit again; Odesa toll rises** — Ukraine struck the
Crimean (Kerch) Bridge again overnight into 19 November and traffic
was halted. Russian strikes on Odesa killed four people and injured
13.
— Ukrainian General Staff statements and wire reporting, 19 Nov.

**6. Dow falls 561 as Fed hints at hike** — US markets closed mixed on
Wednesday 18 November: the Dow fell 561.25 points (−1.11%) to
50,198.17 and the S&P 500 slipped for a fifth straight fall. Nvidia's
third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion beat forecasts, yet its shares
fell 2.9% as hawkish Federal Reserve minutes lifted bets on a December
rate hike.
— Market reports, 18–19 Nov.

**7. Voyager 1 passes one light-day** — NASA's Voyager 1 reached one
light-day from Earth on Wednesday 18 November 2026 — 16.1 billion
miles (25.9 billion km) out, the first human-made object to do so. Its
signals now take a full 24 hours to arrive.
— NASA JPL, Smithsonian Magazine and ScienceAlert, 18–19 Nov.

**8. Van Gogh sells for record $190.4m** — Van Gogh's *Châtaigniers en
fleurs* (1890) sold at Sotheby's New York on Wednesday 18 November for
$190.4 million with fees, smashing the previous Van Gogh record of
$117.2 million.
— Sotheby's results and arts reporting, 18–19 Nov.

**9. COP31 deadlocked with two days left** — The COP31 climate summit
in Antalya remained deadlocked with two days left, with 21 issues
unresolved; the OPEC secretary-general defended fossil fuels.
— UN News and summit reporting, 19 Nov.

**10. Prague fills streets for free media** — Hundreds of thousands of
people protested in Prague in defence of media independence, among the
largest demonstrations in the Czech Republic in years.
— Czech and European reporting, 19 Nov.

**11. Cyclone Akvan nears India coast** — Cyclone Akvan was forecast
to cross the Indian coast between Machilipatnam and Puri, on the
Andhra Pradesh–Odisha shoreline.
— India Meteorological Department advisories via 19 Nov reports.

**12. India, Japan open 2+2 talks in Tokyo** — India and Japan opened
2+2 defence and foreign ministers' talks in Tokyo, with India's
External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar calling defence and security
ties a key pillar of the relationship.
— Indian and Japanese official reporting, 19 Nov.
